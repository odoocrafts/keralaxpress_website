About Kerala Xpress

We are Kerala's most trusted logistics partner for e-commerce sellers.

Kerala Xpress Logistics was founded with a single mission: to empower local e-commerce sellers, retailers, and D2C brands with enterprise-grade delivery capabilities. We understand the unique geography, consumer behavior, and market dynamics of Kerala better than anyone else. Whether you are shipping electronics, fashion, or essential goods, we are the trusted courier service in Kerala that helps you scale.


With our robust statewide network, advanced route-optimization technology, and dedicated local fulfillment centers, we ensure your packages are delivered safely, securely, and on time.
